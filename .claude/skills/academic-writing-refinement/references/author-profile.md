# Author Writing Profile

Version: 0.1.0

## Basis and scope

Qualitative observations from the imported Proposal and selected Chapter 1 review commits, approved as a preliminary interpretation by the author on 2026-11-27. These are mixed-provenance, author-reviewed samples; Git authorship alone does not establish who composed the words. No corpus statistics or detector validation were performed. Preferences below are descriptive, not mechanical rules.

| Dimension | Observed tendency | Editorial implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vocabulary | Ordinary English with domain-specific terms | Prefer precise, familiar words; avoid unnecessary rhetorical embellishment |
| Sentence structure | Direct syntax, sometimes with long lists or accumulated technical detail | Retain understandable constructions; split sentences only for clarity |
| Grammar | Historical drafts contain occasional agreement, article and verb-form errors | Correct errors that affect correctness; do not reproduce errors as style |
| Transitions | Familiar connections such as However, Although, Therefore, For example | Keep when they convey a real relationship; no banned-word list |
| Explanation | General concept followed by a concrete engineering example | Keep relevant examples; request missing examples from the author |
| Terminology | Repeated use of established technical labels | Preserve terms rather than varying them for decoration |
| Tone | Formal and direct; engineer explaining a technical topic | Avoid unnecessary sophistication; retain meaningful qualifications |
| Scope | Redundant modifiers are sometimes removed during review | Remove only when redundant; research-scope changes require approval |

## Academic boundaries

Research accuracy, claim strength, citations, defined terms, and original reasoning take priority over style. A historical example may use outdated methodology, terminology or assumptions. Do not carry those facts forward. Do not infer a preference from a single edit or treat a substantive research correction as a stylistic rule.

## Updating the profile

Record author-approved corrections with the original and revised excerpt, commit or source reference, and a brief explanation. Distinguish verified author-written material from AI-assisted or uncertain material. Change this profile only on explicit request.
