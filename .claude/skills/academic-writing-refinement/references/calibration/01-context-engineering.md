# Calibration 01 — Context engineering

Source: author–ChatGPT calibration conversation, 2026-11-27. Exercise, not thesis text. AI draft followed by author revision as submitted; not independent writing. Original spelling and punctuation retained.

## AI draft

Context engineering has emerged as an important practice in the development of LLM-based applications. Rather than focusing solely on the formulation of effective prompts, context engineering encompasses the systematic selection, organisation, and management of information provided to a language model during task execution.

In the context of software engineering, such information may include repository structures, source code, technical documentation, project-specific conventions, and historical execution records. By providing relevant contextual information, developers can support the model's understanding of the development environment and the requirements associated with a particular task.

However, the availability of extensive contextual information does not necessarily translate into improved agent performance. The relevance and organisation of the supplied information remain important considerations when designing LLM-based development workflows.

## Author revision

Context engineering is one of the important practice in the development of LLM-based applications. Beyond focusing on formulating effective prompts, context engineering emphasizes the management of information such as repository structures, source code, technical documentation, project-specific conventions and historical execution records that provided to LLM during task execution. By providing relevant contextual information, LLM can understand the development environment and the requirements associated with a particular task, then generate the output that closer to developer's expectation.
However, providing more contextual information does not guarantee improvement of agent performance. The relevance of the context provided to coding agent is an important consideration when designing the development workflow
