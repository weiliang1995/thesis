## AI draft

Yao et al. (2023) introduced ReAct as an approach that integrates reasoning and action within language model-based agents. Instead of relying exclusively on internal reasoning, the proposed approach enables language models to interact with external environments and incorporate observations into subsequent reasoning steps.

The experimental findings demonstrated that combining reasoning and action can improve performance across several tasks involving question answering and decision-making. These findings suggest that interaction with external environments can complement the reasoning capabilities of language models.

However, the effectiveness of such an approach in software engineering cannot be directly established from these findings. Software development tasks may involve additional considerations, including repository dependencies, project-specific requirements, and validation procedures. Consequently, further investigation is required to understand how reasoning-and-action mechanisms operate within complex software development workflows.
