## Evidence and limits
Eight frontier models on SWE-bench Verified. Input dominates token usage; repeated runs on identical issues can differ up to 30-fold. Human-rated difficulty has weak alignment with actual usage. Higher observed consumption is not consistently associated with better accuracy. The study is directly relevant to token variability and complexity, but observational cost–accuracy patterns do not identify the causal effect of a larger budget. Human difficulty labels are not the thesis's enterprise duration categories.
