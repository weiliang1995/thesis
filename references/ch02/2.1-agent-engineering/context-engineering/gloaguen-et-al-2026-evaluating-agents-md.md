## Intended role in Chapter 2.1.3

Direct empirical evidence concerning repository-level context files and their potential costs; also relevant to Sections 2.2–2.4.

## Preliminary reading notes

The authors evaluate generated context files on SWE-bench tasks and developer-committed files on a separate issue collection. The v3 abstract reports no general improvement in task success and an average inference-cost increase exceeding 20%. It also distinguishes useful instructions about non-standard practices from repository overviews.

Before final citation, examine the task collections, agent/model configurations, cost definition, comparisons and limitations. Do not extrapolate this result to all context engineering techniques or to Trade-Harness.
