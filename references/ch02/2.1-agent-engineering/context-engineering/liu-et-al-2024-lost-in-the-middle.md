## Preliminary reading notes

The experiments use multi-document question answering and key-value retrieval. Performance often declines when relevant information is positioned in the middle of a long input rather than near its boundaries.

This supports a distinction between context capacity and effective context utilisation. It is not a coding-agent study; do not infer a universal context-length threshold or an enterprise software-quality effect.
