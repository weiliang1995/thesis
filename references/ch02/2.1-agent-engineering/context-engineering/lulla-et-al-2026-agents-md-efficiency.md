## Intended role in Chapter 2.1.3

Empirical evidence on the operational efficiency of coding agents with repository-level context files; also relevant to Sections 2.2 and 2.4.

## Preliminary reading notes

The authors compare agents with and without AGENTS.md across 124 pull requests in ten repositories. The abstract reports a 28.64% reduction in median runtime and a 16.58% reduction in output-token consumption, with comparable task completion behaviour.

Before final citation, examine the execution protocol, model, statistical analysis, token definitions and completion measure. Output tokens are not equivalent to total tokens or inference cost; the results need not contradict Gloaguen et al. (2026).
