## Preliminary reading notes

RepoCoder combines a similarity-based retriever with a code language model in repeated retrieval and generation. Its abstract reports gains exceeding 10% over an in-file completion baseline across evaluated settings, and improvements over a simpler retrieval-augmented approach.

The task is code completion, not end-to-end issue resolution or enterprise delivery. Verify the exact metrics, units and benchmark settings before using numerical results.
