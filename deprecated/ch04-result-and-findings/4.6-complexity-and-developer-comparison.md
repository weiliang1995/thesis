Simple tasks have almost identical mean lead times in the two conditions (1.00 days for baseline, 1.01 days for Trade-Harness). Trade-Harness has a slightly higher mean E2E pass rate and a higher mean LLM cost in this subgroup.

For medium tasks, Trade-Harness has a somewhat lower mean lead time and a higher E2E pass rate, but also higher mean defect density and cost per task. In the complex subgroup, Trade-Harness again has a lower mean lead time and higher E2E pass rate, while defect density and token cost are higher. Only six baseline and seven Trade-Harness tasks are complex; the subgroup cannot support strong generalizations.

Across all three complexity bands, Trade-Harness has higher mean final E2E pass rates and higher mean LLM costs. The defect-density pattern is mixed. These descriptive differences should not be interpreted as causal workflow effects.

### 4.6.3 E2E Test-Script Correction Loops

A correction loop is a revision of generated E2E test code following a failed run, followed by rerunning the tests. The initial test execution is not counted. Both workflows received a three-loop instruction.

**Table 4.14. E2E test-script correction loops by workflow**

| Measure | Baseline | Trade-Harness |
|---|---:|---:|
| Valid tasks (n) | 300 | 246 |
| Mean correction loops / task | 1.00 | 0.75 |
| Median correction loops / task | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| Tasks with 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 loops | 114 / 105 / 60 / 13 | 122 / 76 / 35 / 13 |
| Tasks above 3 loops | 8 | 0 |

The baseline has a higher mean correction-loop count. Eight baseline tasks exceeded the instructed three-loop maximum; none in Trade-Harness did. These are recorded as process deviations, not proof of any particular context, memory or software-quality mechanism.

### 4.6.4 Cross-Metric Pattern

The overall results show almost identical development lead times, higher mean E2E pass rate and lower mean task-level defect density with Trade-Harness, and higher LLM cost. Complexity-specific results vary, including a higher task-level defect density for Trade-Harness in medium and complex groups.

No inferential test is imposed in this draft. Task assignment is nonrandomized, complex tasks are few, and developers contribute repeated observations.
