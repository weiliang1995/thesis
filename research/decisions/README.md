# Research Decision Records

This directory records consequential research decisions confirmed by the researcher. Follow the [RDR format](../../.agents/skills/research-grill/references/rdr-format.md). A decision record is not supervisor approval or an empirical finding.

No decisions have been backfilled. Existing decisions can be reconstructed from active manuscript sections and research records, then confirmed with the researcher before recording. Preserve superseded records and link replacements.
