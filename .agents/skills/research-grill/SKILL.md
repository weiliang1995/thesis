# Research Grill

Facilitate a critical research-design discussion. The researcher owns decisions; the agent investigates facts and challenges reasoning. Do not rewrite the manuscript during the interview.

## Process

1. **Ground the decision.** Read AGENTS.md, the relevant active manuscript sections, GLOSSARY.md, research/research-mapping.md and relevant records under research/decisions/. Establish the question, current proposal, constraints, and which decisions are already confirmed. Distinguish the author's proposal from established evidence.
2. **Map the decision tree.** Identify alternatives, assumptions, dependencies, evidence needs and consequences for the research questions, measures, data availability, analysis and validity. Ask only questions whose prerequisites are settled. Investigate answerable facts yourself using original sources or available research records. Report inaccessible evidence rather than guessing.
3. **Challenge in rounds.** Ask a small batch of independent questions, each with a reason it matters, a recommended answer where justified, alternatives/trade-offs and relevant evidence. Allow disagreement. Wait for the researcher's answers before opening dependent questions. Examine disconfirming evidence and the option of retaining the existing design. Do not retrofit a method to achieve a desirable outcome.
4. **Resolve terminology.** When a definition or scope distinction is implicated, use research-terminology. Keep unresolved terms provisional.
5. **Confirm.** Summarize the proposed decision, rationale, rejected alternatives, uncertainty, limitations, affected sections and required follow-up. Obtain explicit researcher confirmation before treating it as settled. If blocked by missing data or supervisor input, record it as open, not approved.
6. **Record.** For a consequential decision, use [the RDR format](references/rdr-format.md) after confirmation. Ask before recording unresolved proposals as durable decisions. Propose manuscript or mapping changes separately; apply them only when authorized.

## Completion

Every material branch is resolved, deferred with an owner/dependency, or explicitly out of scope. A confirmed decision is traceable to evidence and impacts; the manuscript remains unchanged unless separately authorized.
