# Research Terminology

Maintain a precise shared language for the thesis. This is definition work, not a passive consistency review. For a read-only audit, use thesis-review's terminology axis when the researcher invokes it.

## Process

1. Read the [project map](../../../AGENTS.md), [thesis standards](../../docs/thesis.md) and [glossary](../../docs/glossary.md). Use the [thesis structure](../../docs/architecture.md) to locate definitions and usages, and consult cited original literature and relevant [decision records](../../docs/decisions/README.md). If the glossary or decision records are absent or empty, state that and establish the current state from available sources. Identify what is agreed, provisional or contradictory.
2. Challenge the term: what does it refer to, what does it exclude, where does it overlap with other concepts, and what is a concrete counterexample? Distinguish a literature-based conceptual definition from this study's operational definition. Identify viable alternatives and evidence, including variation in definitions across sources. Do not invent artificial boundaries or assume current manuscript wording is approved.
3. For a measured construct, check the metric, numerator/denominator, unit, scope, observation window, start/end events and aggregation where applicable. Mark unspecified fields as pending rather than inferring them. Distinguish canonical naming from quoted source vocabulary.
4. Present a proposed glossary entry using [the glossary format](references/glossary-format.md), with precise source locations, alternative terms, limitations and affected manuscript sections. Distinguish source wording from proposed thesis usage. If a term change would alter study design or interpretation, route the decision through research-grill before confirming the definition.
5. Follow the [glossary maintenance instructions](../../docs/glossary.md) when recording a definition. Preserve uncertainty and link superseded decisions where applicable. Propose cross-chapter manuscript changes separately and apply them with the researcher's authorization.

## Completion

A confirmed term has a traceable definition and applicable operational details, or explicit pending fields. Conflicts and affected sections are surfaced. Confirmation settles vocabulary only, does not validate a measure or authorize manuscript edits, and makes no research decision implicitly.
