# Glossary Entry Format

GLOSSARY.md is the shared vocabulary of the thesis, not the bibliography, methodology or a decision log. Keep entries compact; include operational details when they prevent ambiguity and link the full method or confirmed RDR.

## Canonical term

- **Status:** Confirmed | Provisional (only if explicitly requested)
- **Conceptual definition:** Meaning and scope; identify literature-based versus study-specific language.
- **Operational definition:** How this study observes or measures it; include only applicable units, numerator/denominator, time boundaries, inclusion/exclusion and aggregation. Mark unresolved details as pending; use N/A for non-measured concepts.
- **Distinctions / aliases:** Related terms, legitimate overlaps, non-equivalent terms and allowed variants.
- **Source:** Original literature with location and/or active manuscript path and heading; link confirmed RDR when applicable.
- **Usage notes:** Constraints on claims or interpretation if necessary.

Use exact terminology from approved sources where appropriate without copying extended passages. A glossary entry does not establish empirical validity. If sources conflict, document the distinction and the researcher's chosen usage. Keep historical records in Git or linked RDRs rather than creating competing canonical entries.
