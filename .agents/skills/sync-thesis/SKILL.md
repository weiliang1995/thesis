# Sync Thesis

Use after a source of truth has been confirmed or updated. Accept a document, entry, document diff or chapter scope. With no narrower scope, use current confirmed project documents and the active manuscript.

1. **Read the confirmed sources.** Start with the [project map](../../../AGENTS.md), [revision standards](../../docs/thesis.md#revision-and-source-of-truth), and [thesis structure](../../docs/architecture.md). Identify the confirmed definitions, standards and decisions that apply. If the researcher supplies a report or finding ID, use it to locate the confirmed source and affected passages. Follow the revision standards for conflicts and unresolved evidence.
2. **Update the affected manuscript.** Use the structure map and, where relevant, the [research mapping](../../docs/research-mapping.md) to find dependent sections. Search for old and current terminology and inspect each occurrence in context. Apply the confirmed meaning in the active front matter and chapters, including relevant tables, captions and cross-references. Update affected citations or bibliography entries when the approved correction is backed by the verified publication. For a narrower request, summarize affected passages outside that scope.
3. **Check synchronization.** Compare the diff with the confirmed sources. Recheck affected definitions, indicators, citation associations and links. Search for remaining conflicts within scope. Report what was synchronized, with links to the sources and changed passages, and identify any open items. Keep this skill's edits scoped to the manuscript and related citation entries.
