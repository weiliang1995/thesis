# Axis: Thesis Alignment

## Procedure

1. Extract the current title, scope, problem statements, objectives (ROs), research questions (RQs), literature themes/gaps and design rationale, methodology, operational measures, data sources, analysis plan and available results/discussion/conclusions. Use actual headings and identifiers. Distinguish the active manuscript, confirmed decisions, plans and historical drafts. Use the [research mapping](../../../docs/research-mapping.md) to find candidate links and verify each against the manuscript.
2. Construct a bidirectional traceability matrix from actual manuscript content. Use one row per research question or meaningful sub-question, with multiple linked items where needed. Include unmapped objectives/problems and any unclaimed measures/results. Do not force one-to-one mappings. Mark missing, pending and not applicable explicitly.
3. Check semantic fit at each link: does the literature establish the problem and justify the gap? Does the objective address the problem? Can the RQ be answered by the design, measures and obtainable data? Do the units, inclusion criteria, comparison groups and analysis match? Do the findings actually answer the RQ, and are conclusions bounded by design limitations and observed results?
4. Look across chapters for contradictions in participants, timing, task complexity, treatment/baseline, metrics, collection, analysis, validity, and the claimed intervention. Inspect formulas and aggregation definitions where applicable, but distinguish logical alignment from independent statistical validation. Check that causal language is warranted by the design; do not presume quasi-experimental evidence establishes causation or that a null result is failure.
5. Check reverse traceability: every substantive metric or result should have an explicit research purpose; each conclusion should link to results; each RQ should have a credible planned or completed answer. Separate missing material in a planned future chapter from a contradiction or unjustified commitment. Identify blocked links without silently filling gaps.

## Completion

Account for every in-scope problem, objective, RQ, measure and available result in forward and reverse checks. Return the matrix and actionable broken links; identify unavailable chapters or data. Updating the persistent research mapping or manuscript requires separate authorization.
