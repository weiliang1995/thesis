#### 4.2.1 Delivery Efficiency Metrics

Delivery efficiency measures the delivery lead time, from Jira issue transition to In Progress to successful feature deployment in the test environment.

$$T = T_{completion} - T_{initiation}$$

where $T_{completion}$ is the timestamp of proceed to QA staging, and $T_{initiation}$ is the timestamp when Jira issue transitions to In Progress. The unit is days.
