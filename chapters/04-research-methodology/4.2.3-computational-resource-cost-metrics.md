#### 4.2.3 Computational Resource Cost Metrics

Cost metrics evaluate the economic footprint and the structural density of token consumption per software artefact.

LOC per Million Tokens: Evaluate the generation efficiency by measuring how many net lines of production code are integrated into the repository per one million tokens consumed.

$$L = \frac{\mathrm{\Delta}LOC}{{Tokens}_{total}}*1,000,000$$

where LOC is the line of code and L is the LOC per million tokens.

Token Cost per Issue: Calculates the average financial expense generated by the AI Agent associated with resolving a single Jira Issue in US Dollar.

$$C_{issue} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i = 1}^{N}C_{issue,i}$$

where $C_{issue}\ $is the arithmetic mean cost per issue in US Dollar. N is the total number of Jira issues completed within the observation cohort. $C_{issue,\ i}$ is the total cost for an individual issue *i*.
