## 4. Research Methodology

This chapter describes the methodology used to evaluate Trade-Harness against a developer-managed AI-assisted workflow over a 12-week period of normal front-end development. Section 4.1 presents the quasi-experimental design, task allocation, research variables, and experimental procedure. Section 4.2 defines the engineering measures, their data sources, and the preparation and analysis of task-level records. Section 4.3 discusses threats to the validity of the comparison and the measures used to reduce their influence.
