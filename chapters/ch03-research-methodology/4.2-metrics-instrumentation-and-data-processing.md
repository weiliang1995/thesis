### 4.2 Data Collection and Analysis

This section defines how delivery efficiency, software quality, and computational resource efficiency and cost are measured for each feature-development task. The study uses an existing AI-development dashboard that consolidates Jira, GitLab, and enterprise LLM gateway records, together with task-specific E2E test reports saved in the development repository. Sections 4.2.1–4.2.4 describe the measures and their data sources; Section 4.2.5 explains how the records are prepared, compared, and interpreted.
