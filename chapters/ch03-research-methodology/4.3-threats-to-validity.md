### 4.3 Threats to Validity

The study compares two AI-assisted development workflows in a working front-end team rather than in a fully controlled laboratory setting. This makes the results relevant to actual development practice, but it also introduces factors that may influence the observed differences between the workflows. Following the validity considerations discussed by Runeson and Höst (2009), this section examines threats arising from the research design, the interpretation of the measures, the study setting, and the analysis. Each subsection describes the precautions taken and the limitations that remain.
