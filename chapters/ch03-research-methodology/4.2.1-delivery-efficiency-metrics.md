#### 4.2.1 Delivery Efficiency Metrics

Delivery efficiency is measured using development lead time for each task. The measurement begins when the Jira issue transitions to *In Progress* and ends when it moves to the intermediate *Development Complete* status, indicating that the developer has finished the work and handed it over to the next stage. Waiting for QA activities or deployment to a test environment after this transition is not included.

$$T_{lead,i} = T_{dev\_complete,i} - T_{in\_progress,i}$$

where $T_{in\_progress,i}$ is the timestamp when task $i$ enters *In Progress* and $T_{dev\_complete,i}$ is its transition timestamp to *Development Complete*. Both timestamps are obtained from Jira issue history. The difference is reported in elapsed days. This measure represents the duration of the developer-owned delivery stage, rather than active coding time or AI generation time alone.
