### 1.1 Research Background

The application of AI in software engineering has increased in recent years, including front-end engineering. Previously, they are mainly used in information retrieval, data analytics, decision-making and code suggestions. Now, AI coding assistants are becoming autonomous agents that are able to support multiple stages, such as plan, execute tasks and validate their outcomes (Acharya et al., 2025).

Although the regular AI coding assistants and AI agent can reduce human effort in software development, but their level of autonomy, context dependency and functionality are different. AI coding assistants can generate or provide code suggestions based on the prompt given by developers; AI agent can involve in other part of Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) such as requirement analysis, task decomposition, implementation, testing, validation and code review.

In this section, we will discuss the evolution of agentic AI in software engineering, its applications in front-end development and the limitations of LLMs, which motivate our evaluation of the orchestration process.
