### 1.1 Research Background

AI coding tools have expanded from providing code suggestions to supporting multiple activities in software development. Modern coding agents can interpret instructions, examine source code, make changes, execute tests, and revise their outputs based on feedback (Acharya et al., 2025).

However, the ability to perform individual development activities does not necessarily mean that an AI agent can consistently follow the development process of a particular project. In enterprise systems, developers must also consider repository dependencies, project-specific conventions, and requirements for testing and review.

This section discusses the use of agentic AI in software engineering and front-end development, the constraints involved in applying these tools to complex projects, and the approach adopted by Trade-Harness to coordinate agent execution.
