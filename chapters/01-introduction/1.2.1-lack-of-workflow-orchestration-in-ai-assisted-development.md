#### 1.2.1 Workflow Coordination and Delivery Efficiency

Existing studies have examined how AI coding tools support developers in code generation, debugging, and other development activities. However, improvements in individual coding activities do not necessarily reflect improvements in overall software delivery efficiency.

In conventional prompt-based AI-assisted development, developers are responsible for coordinating task execution, providing relevant context, and validating the generated outputs. Otten et al. (2025) reported challenges in using generative AI tools for complex coding and debugging tasks, where iterative prompting is commonly involved. Such interactions may introduce additional coordination effort beyond code implementation.

Structured agent orchestration workflows provide an alternative approach by organising development activities into defined stages. While this may reduce the need for manual coordination, additional planning and validation steps may also increase execution time. This raises the question of whether structured orchestration can reduce delivery lead time compared with conventional prompt-based workflows.
