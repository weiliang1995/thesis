## 1. Introduction

AI coding assistants now support more than code generation. They can also help developers plan, modify, test, and review code. In enterprise front-end development, however, completing a task depends on how requirements, repository context, implementation, and validation are coordinated across the development process. This chapter introduces Trade-Harness as a structured agent orchestration workflow and explains why its effectiveness needs to be evaluated against a conventional developer-managed prompt-based workflow. The study focuses on delivery efficiency, software quality, and computational cost.
