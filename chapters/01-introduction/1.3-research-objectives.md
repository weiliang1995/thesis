### 1.3 Research Objectives

The main objective of this study is to evaluate the effectiveness and trade-offs of Trade-Harness as a structured agent orchestration workflow compared with a conventional prompt-based AI-assisted development workflow in an enterprise front-end development environment.

The specific objectives are:

**RO1:** To evaluate the delivery efficiency of structured agent orchestration compared with conventional prompt-based AI-assisted development, using delivery lead time as the primary measure.

**RO2:** To examine the software quality outcomes of structured agent orchestration compared with the conventional prompt-based workflow, based on defect density and integration testing pass rate.

**RO3:** To assess the computational resource efficiency and associated cost of structured agent orchestration relative to the conventional prompt-based workflow, using lines of code per million tokens and token cost per resolved issue.

The evaluation also considers task complexity to examine whether the observed differences vary across different types of development tasks.
