### 1.3 Research Objectives

The main objective of this study is to evaluate the effectiveness of Trade-Harness, a structured agent orchestration workflow, compared with a conventional developer-managed prompt-based AI-assisted workflow in an enterprise front-end development environment. The evaluation considers three outcomes of the overall development workflow: delivery efficiency, software quality, and computational cost.

The specific objectives are:

1. To compare delivery lead time between Trade-Harness and the conventional prompt-based workflow.
2. To compare software quality between the two workflows using defect density and integration testing pass rate.
3. To compare computational resource efficiency and cost between the two workflows using lines of code per million tokens and token cost per resolved issue.

Task complexity is considered when interpreting the comparisons, rather than treated as a separate research objective.
