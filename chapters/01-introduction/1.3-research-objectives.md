### 1.3 Research Objectives

To address the challenges mentioned above, this study introduces Trade-Harness as a structured orchestration workflow for a cryptocurrency exchange front-end system. The framework is designed to orchestrate AI agents through predefined workflows and execution rules, it is expected to standardize the workflow of software development activities, in order to improve the delivery efficiency, code quality and reduce token cost.

To achieve the objective, the sub-objectives are as follows:

• To determine whether structured orchestration reduces delivery lead time by minimizing manual workflow coordination, task interpretation, and repeated context reconstruction.

• To examine whether structured orchestration improves software quality by supporting repository-aware context handling, staged validation, and consistency with architectural constraints.

• To assess whether structured orchestration improves cost-effectiveness by reducing redundant context loading and improving the efficiency of token usage during front-end development tasks.
