### 1.4 Research Questions

This study compares Trade-Harness with a conventional developer-managed prompt-based AI-assisted workflow using real front-end development tasks. Three research questions are formulated based on the research objectives.

**RQ1:** How does delivery lead time differ between tasks completed using Trade-Harness and those completed using the conventional prompt-based workflow?

**RQ2:** How do defect density and integration testing pass rate compare between the two workflows?

**RQ3:** How do lines of code per million tokens and token cost per resolved issue compare between the two workflows?
