### 1.4 Research Questions

This study compares Trade-Harness with a conventional developer-managed prompt-based AI-assisted workflow using real front-end development tasks. The research questions correspond to the three evaluation outcomes defined in the research objectives.

1. How does delivery lead time differ between tasks completed using Trade-Harness and those completed using the conventional prompt-based workflow?
2. How do defect density and integration testing pass rate compare between the two workflows?
3. How do lines of code per million tokens and token cost per resolved issue compare between the two workflows?
