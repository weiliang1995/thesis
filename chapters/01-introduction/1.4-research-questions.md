### 1.4 Research Questions

This study compares a structured agent orchestration workflow, implemented as Trade-Harness, with a conventional prompt-based workflow in front-end development. The following research questions are formulated based on the research objectives.

**RQ1:** How does delivery lead time differ between the structured agent orchestration workflow and the conventional prompt-based workflow?

**RQ2:** How do defect density and E2E testing pass rate differ between the two workflows?

**RQ3:** How do token consumption per issue and changed lines of code per million tokens differ between the two workflows?
