#### 1.6.1 Research Delimitations

This study is limited to front-end development tasks within an existing cryptocurrency exchange system. Back-end development, mobile applications, and major architectural redesign are excluded. Development tasks are carried out within the existing system architecture and repository structure.

The evaluation focuses on two development workflows: the prompt-based workflow and the structured agent orchestration workflow implemented as Trade-Harness. Both workflows use Claude Code CLI with Claude Sonnet 4.6. The study does not evaluate other language models or coding agents.

The study involves two front-end developers working on assigned development tasks. The evaluation compares the overall outcomes of the two workflows rather than the individual contributions of orchestration mechanisms such as context management, memory, and validation.
