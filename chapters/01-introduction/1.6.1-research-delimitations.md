#### 1.6.1 Research Delimitations

This study focuses on front-end development tasks within a cryptocurrency exchange system built with React and TypeScript, consisting of a main repository and several component libraries. Back-end and mobile application development, system architecture redesign, and competitor research are outside its scope. Existing architectural constraints and cross-repository dependencies are considered when carrying out front-end tasks.

The comparison is limited to two AI-assisted development workflows: a conventional developer-managed prompt-based workflow and Trade-Harness, which adds structured agent orchestration. The study does not compare different language models or AI coding tools. Both conditions use Claude Code CLI with Claude Sonnet 4.6. The study involves two developers who use both workflows on assigned tasks to reduce, although not eliminate, variation associated with individual developer practices.
