#### 1.6.1 Research Delimitations

This research focuses primarily on the front-end system of a cryptocurrency exchange, built with React and TypeScript, consisting of a main repository and several component libraries. All tasks within the study revolve around this front-end system; back-end development, mobile development, architecture, or competitor research are excluded. This study only compares two workflows and does not compare different LLM models or AI tools. Furthermore, while the study involves two developers, they utilize both workflows on tasks to minimize individual developer preferences bias.
