#### 1.6.2 Research Limitations

This study is conducted in an active software development environment rather than a controlled laboratory setting. Since development tasks are completed only once as part of normal project delivery, identical tasks cannot be evaluated under both workflows. Although tasks are grouped according to their estimated development effort and characteristics, differences in task requirements, dependencies, and complexity may still influence the observed results.

The study involves only two front-end developers who use both workflows. Differences in their experience, familiarity with the codebase, and working practices may affect development outcomes. Although both developers participate in the two workflow conditions, the small sample size limits the extent to which individual differences can be accounted for.

Another limitation is the non-deterministic behaviour of large language models. Even when the same model and workflow instructions are used, coding agents may produce different outputs or follow different execution paths. This variability cannot be fully controlled and may affect the consistency of the results.

Finally, the study is conducted within a single cryptocurrency exchange front-end system using a specific coding agent and language model. The findings may therefore not apply directly to other software systems, development teams, or AI coding tools. The results should be interpreted within the technical and organisational context of the study.
