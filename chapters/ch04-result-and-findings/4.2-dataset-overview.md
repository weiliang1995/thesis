## 4.2 Dataset Overview

The Jira issue is the unit of observation. Eligible front-end tasks were selected from normal development work and classified before implementation as simple (estimated effort ≤ 1 working day), medium (> 1 and ≤ 4 working days), or complex (> 4 working days). The technical lead assigned tasks and workflow conditions without randomization. Each developer worked with both workflows on different tasks.
