## 4.3 Delivery Efficiency Results (RQ1)

> **Provisional results (dataset snapshot reviewed 2026-11-22):** 548 Jira issues, ten developers, 302 baseline tasks and 246 Trade-Harness tasks. The final research-sample definition remains open. Findings below are descriptive; tasks were not randomly assigned.

Development lead time is reported in **elapsed days** from the first *In Progress* transition to the final *Development Complete* transition of each Jira issue. It reflects developer-stage elapsed time rather than active coding time.

### 4.3.1 Overall Lead Time

**Table 4.4. Development lead time (elapsed days)**

| Statistic | Baseline | Trade-Harness |
|---|---:|---:|
| Valid tasks (n) | 302 | 246 |
| Mean | 1.72 | 1.72 |
| Median | 0.88 | 0.88 |
| Standard deviation | 2.06 | 2.14 |
| IQR | 2.65 | 1.95 |
| Range | 0.05–11.25 | 0.05–11.26 |

The two workflows have almost identical mean lead time: **1.72 days** for the baseline and **1.72 days** for Trade-Harness, an observed difference of only **-0.001 days**. Their medians are both **0.88 days**. These figures do not show a meaningful overall lead-time separation in the current dataset. The averages exceed the medians in both conditions, reflecting a right-skewed distribution.

### 4.3.2 Lead Time by Task Complexity

**Table 4.5. Lead time by task complexity (days)**

| Complexity | Baseline n | Baseline mean | Baseline median | Trade-Harness n | Trade-Harness mean | Trade-Harness median | Median difference (TH − B) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Simple | 232 | 1.00 | 0.76 | 189 | 1.01 | 0.77 | 0.01 |
| Medium | 64 | 3.65 | 3.09 | 50 | 3.54 | 3.08 | -0.02 |
| Complex | 6 | 8.76 | 9.08 | 7 | 7.82 | 7.98 | -1.10 |

For simple tasks, mean and median lead times are nearly the same across workflows. Trade-Harness has a slightly lower median for medium tasks and a larger observed difference for complex tasks. The complex-task comparison is based on only six and seven tasks, so the difference could be strongly influenced by task characteristics and developer allocation.
