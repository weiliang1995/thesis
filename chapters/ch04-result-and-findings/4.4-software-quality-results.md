## 4.4 Software Quality Results (RQ2)

> **Provisional results:** Based on 290 issues and ten developers in the current dataset. The final sample design and defect extraction cutoff require confirmation. Results are descriptive, not causal estimates.

This section compares final E2E test pass rate and defect density between the developer-managed baseline and Trade-Harness. Final E2E pass rate is calculated separately for each task against its applicable JSON test cases; failed and unexecuted cases are retained in the denominator. Defect density uses confirmed Jira-linked defects divided by the final production-code changed LOC (added LOC plus absolute deleted LOC), expressed per 1,000 changed lines.

### 4.4.1 Final E2E Test Pass Rate

**Table 4.7. Final task-level E2E pass rate by workflow (percentage)**

| Statistic | Baseline | Trade-Harness |
|---|---:|---:|
| Usable issues (n) | 158 | 130 |
| Mean | 90.79 | 92.83 |
| Median | 100.00 | 100.00 |
| Sample standard deviation | 12.11 | 11.37 |
| Interquartile range | 16.67 | 14.29 |
| Minimum–maximum | 50.00–100.00 | 60.00–100.00 |

The mean task-level pass rate was **90.79%** for the baseline and **92.83%** for Trade-Harness, a difference of **2.05 percentage points**. Both workflows had a median of **100.00%**, so the difference appears in the lower-scoring tasks rather than in the median outcome. The IQR was 16.67 percentage points for the baseline and 14.29 for Trade-Harness. The minimum observed task-level pass rates were 50.00% and 60.00%, respectively.

The baseline contributed 852 applicable cases across 158 task reports (768 passed, 65 failed and 19 unexecuted). Trade-Harness contributed 747 cases across 130 task reports (694 passed, 42 failed and 11 unexecuted). These raw totals are included for context; the primary comparison is the **mean of task-level pass rates**, not the pooled percentage of cases, because task documents contain different numbers of cases. One baseline and one Trade-Harness issue lacked a usable E2E report and were excluded from this outcome.

### 4.4.2 Defect Density

**Table 4.8. Confirmed defects and defect density by workflow**

| Minimum–maximum | 0.00–13.79 | 0.00–7.41 |
| Pooled defects / 1,000 changed LOC (supplementary) | 0.431 | 0.348 |

The mean task-level density was **0.93 defects per 1,000 changed LOC** for the baseline and **0.72** for Trade-Harness. Both medians were zero, reflecting many issues with no confirmed linked defects. The pooled densities, 0.431 and 0.348 defects per 1,000 changed LOC, respectively, are separately reported because they weight larger code changes more heavily. Neither pooled density replaces the task-level distribution.

The baseline density range (0.00–13.79) includes higher maximum values than the Trade-Harness range (0.00–7.41). Valid high-density cases are retained even when the production-code change is small. Issue **DBU-2550** has zero attributable changed production LOC, making its task-level density undefined; its other valid outcomes remain in the dataset. A zero recorded defect count does not establish that a delivered feature is defect-free.
