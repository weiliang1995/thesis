## 4.1 Introduction

This chapter presents the findings from the comparison between the developer-managed baseline workflow and Trade-Harness in enterprise front-end development. Both conditions used Claude Code CLI with Claude Sonnet 4.6, and each eligible Jira task was completed once under its assigned workflow condition.

The analysis follows the measurements and data processing procedures specified in the Methodology chapter. Results are presented for development lead time (RQ1), final E2E test pass rate and defect density (RQ2), and changed production-code LOC per million tokens and LLM token cost per resolved issue (RQ3). E2E test-script correction loops are reported as a supplementary process measure.

The chapter begins with dataset characteristics and data availability, then presents overall workflow comparisons and comparisons stratified by task complexity and developer. The findings are described in terms of observed differences rather than causal effects.
