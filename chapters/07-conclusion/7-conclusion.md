## 7. Conclusion

This study evaluates Trade-Harness as a structured orchestration workflow and compares it with existing conventional prompt-based AI-assisted workflows. The study does not attempt to change the behaviour of the language model itself, nor does it evaluate a workflow that can replace developers. Instead, it focuses on whether a standard workflow can find a balance between delivery efficiency, code quality, and token cost.

By collecting engineering metrics in a real-world front-end development environment, this study aims to determine whether Trade-Harness can deliver practical improvements under specific development conditions. The research results are expected to help front-end teams better understand the applicable scenarios for structured agent workflows, including when orchestration workflows are worthwhile, when prompt-based workflows are sufficient, and what costs and limitations need to be considered before adopting agent orchestration.
