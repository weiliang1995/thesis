### 1.3 Research Objectives

The main objective of this study is to evaluate a structured agent orchestration workflow, implemented as Trade-harness, by comparing it with a conventional prompt-based workflow in front-end development. The evaluation focuses on delivery efficiency, software quality and computational efficiency.

The research objectives are:

**RO1:** To compare the delivery efficiency of the two workflows based on delivery lead time.

**RO2:** To evaluate differences in software quality between the two workflows using defect density and E2E testing pass rate.

**RO3:** To compare the computational efficiency of the two workflows based on token consumption and resource usage per development task.

The evaluation also considers task complexity to examine whether the observed differences vary across different types of development tasks.
